Layout: page tabs along the bottom edge — Media, Cut, Edit, Fusion, Color, Fairlight, Deliver. Most editing happens on Edit; exporting on Deliver (render settings on the left, Add to Render Queue, then Render All on the right).

Stable shortcuts: play or pause Space, J K L shuttle, blade B (Edit page), selection A, split at playhead ⌘B, ripple delete ⇧Delete, mark in and out I and O, undo ⌘Z, zoom timeline ⌘+ and ⌘-.

Pitfalls: project frame rate is set in Project Settings (gear icon, bottom right) before editing; render resolution and format are on the Deliver page; a red "Media Offline" means moved files (relink from the Media page).
