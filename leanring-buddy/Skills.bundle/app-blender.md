Layout: 3D viewport in the middle, Outliner top-right, Properties editor bottom-right (render, output, object, modifiers, materials tabs as icons), timeline at the bottom, workspace tabs along the top (Layout, Modeling, Sculpting, Shading…).

Stable shortcuts: grab G, rotate R, scale S (then X, Y, or Z to lock an axis), add ⇧A, delete X, Edit or Object mode Tab, frame selected numpad . , camera view numpad 0, render F12, search F3, toggle sidebar N.

Pitfalls: hover the mouse over the area you want the shortcut to act on; apply scale (⌃A) before modifiers; the active render engine (Eevee or Cycles) is in the Render properties tab.
