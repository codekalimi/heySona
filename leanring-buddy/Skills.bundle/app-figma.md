Layout: toolbar at the top (Move, Frame, Shape, Pen, Text, comments), layers and pages on the left, properties on the right (Design, Prototype tabs), canvas in the middle.

Stable shortcuts: Frame F, Rectangle R, Text T, Pen P, Hand Space-drag, Auto Layout ⇧A, Group ⌘G, Frame selection ⌥⌘G, Component ⌥⌘K, zoom to fit ⇧1, zoom to selection ⇧2, duplicate ⌘D, Quick actions ⌘/.

Pitfalls: Auto Layout spacing and padding live in the right panel; constraints only apply inside frames; variants live in component sets.
